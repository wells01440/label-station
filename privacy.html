Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026.

Data collection

Label Station collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer receives nothing you create or print.

What stays on your Mac

Templates, records, print history, and settings are stored in the app's container on your Mac. They leave your Mac only when you export them, print them, or send them to an MCP client you connected.

Printing

Print jobs go to the printers in System Settings > Printers & Scanners through macOS printing. A job reaches only the printer you chose.

MCP clients

The MCP helper inside the app talks only to the client you configure, over standard input and output on your Mac. That client, and any AI service it uses, receives the templates and records it asks for. Its own privacy policy covers what it does with them.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to Label Station's issues on GitHub.
